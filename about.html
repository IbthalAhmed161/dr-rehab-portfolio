Biography

Building a bridge between bedside care and hospital leadership

Dr. Rehab Mohamed Mohamed Hassan graduated from the Faculty of Physical Therapy at Cairo University in May 2003 with Very Good with Honors (81.27%), beginning a clinical career centered on musculoskeletal and post-surgical rehabilitation. Over the following two decades she deepened her craft with specialized international training, including a course on myofascial release in osteopathy from the Osteopathic College of Ontario.

Her path expanded well beyond the treatment room. At Al‑Ibrahimiya Central Hospital she rose to Head of the Physical Therapy Department and later Vice Director / Deputy Director, while also leading the hospital's Media & Public Relations Office to a first-place ranking nationwide. In 2019 she coordinated the presidential initiative "100 Million Healthy Lives" for the Al‑Ibrahimiya sector, and during the second wave of COVID-19 she led frontline care inside the hospital's isolation ward.

To formalize this administrative expertise, she completed a Postgraduate Diploma in Hospital Management at the Faculty of Commerce, Zagazig University (June 2023, Credit Hour System, GPA 3.87 / 88.38%, Grade B+). In parallel she built a second specialty in clinical nutrition — earning a Clinical Nutrition Diploma from AACME, hands-on training at Zewail City of Science and Technology, and virtual practical training with Better Life Clinics — which she now applies alongside physical therapy at Al‑Zahraa Physical Therapy Center, the private practice she leads in Al Sharqia Governorate.

Rehabilitation is never only about a joint or a muscle — it is about the systems of nutrition, care quality and leadership that support a patient's full recovery.
